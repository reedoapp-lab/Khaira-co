# Blemince Paris - Marketing Material Package

## Brand Overview
**Blemince Paris** is a premium seamless lingerie and everyday essentials brand focused on invisible comfort, effortless confidence, and everlasting elegance. The brand embodies the "Too Glam to Blend In" philosophy, creating pieces that feel like a "second skin" - lightweight, breathable, and beautifully made.

**Key Brand Attributes:**
- Premium seamless lingerie & everyday essentials
- "Invisible comfort" and "second skin feel"
- Focus on confidence and elegance
- 4.9★ rating | 1,200+ happy customers
- Worldwide shipping
- Price range: ~200 PLN
- Core colors: Black, white, elegant neutrals
- Font: Montserrat

## 6. PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE GUIDE

### Overall Aesthetic
- **Mood**: Elegant, comfortable, confident, premium
- **Lighting**: Soft, natural, flattering - no harsh shadows
- **Color Grade**: Clean, true-to-life colors with slight warmth
- **Composition**: Balanced, centered or rule-of-thirds

### Styling Guidelines
- **Wardrobe**: Seamless pieces, neutral colors, minimal patterns
- **Props**: Minimal - perhaps luxury packaging, soft fabrics
- **Models**: Diverse range, natural beauty, confident demeanor
- **Hair/Makeup**: Natural, fresh, not over-styled

### Technical Specifications
- **Image Size**: Web-optimized, fast loading
- **Format**: WebP or optimized JPEG/PNG
- **Aspect Ratios**: 
  - Hero: 16:9 or 4:3
  - Product cards: 1:1 or 4:5
  - Instagram: 4:5 or 1:1
  - Stories: 9:16
