# Blemince Paris - Marketing Quick Reference

## BRAND ESSENCE
**Too Glam to Blend In** | Premium seamless lingerie & everyday essentials
**Core promise**: Invisible comfort, effortless confidence, everlasting elegance
**Key differentiator**: "Second skin feel" - lightweight, breathable, barely noticeable

## TARGET AUDIENCE
- Women who value comfort without sacrificing style
- Ages 25-45
- Seeks quality everyday essentials
- Values elegance and confidence
- Likely follows fashion/lingerie trends on social media

## KEY MESSAGING
- "Invisible comfort. Everlasting elegance."
- "Too Glam to Blend In"
- "Second skin feel"
- "Perfect fit for daily life"
- "Exceptional comfort starts with what you wear closest to your skin"

## VISUAL IDENTITY
**Colors**: 
- Black (#000000) - primary text, buttons, accents
- White (#FFFFFF) - backgrounds, cards
- Could add subtle gold/rose gold accents for premium feel

**Font**: Montserrat (all weights: 400, 500, 600, 700)
- Headers: Uppercase, tight tracking
- Body: Normal weight, 1.4-1.6 line-height

**Imagery Style**: 
- Soft natural lighting
- Clean, premium feel
- "Invisible" aesthetic
- Elegant, confident, not overly stylized

## MUST-HAVE ASSETS (Priority Order)

### 1. Website Hero Banner
- Size: 16:9 or 4:3
- Show: Main hero product (Anti-Gravity Bra or signature piece)
- Text overlay: "Invisible Comfort. Everlasting Elegance." or "Too Glam to Blend In"
- Background: White or soft neutral

### 2. Product Photography (3-5 key products)
- Anti-Gravity Bra (hero product)
- Seamless underwear (basic everyday)
- Any bestselling items
- Style: Product on white + lifestyle/on-model

### 3. Badge Designs
- **SALE** - black rectangle, white text
- **NEW** - black rectangle, white text  
- **Best Seller** - 4.9★ rating + label
- **Out of Stock** - greyed out version

### 4. Button Styles
- **Primary**: White text on black background, 0px border radius
- **Secondary**: Outline or reversed
- States: Normal, hover (grey background), active

### 5. Instagram Templates (3 versions)
- Product showcase with price
- Customer testimonial with rating
- Comfort benefit highlight ("second skin feel")

### 6. Email Newsletter Header
- Logo with tagline "Too Glam to Blend In"
- Navigation links
- Consistent with website design

## CONTENT THEMES (Weekly)

### Monday: New week, new confidence
- Feature a product + comfort benefit

### Wednesday: Mid-week comfort  
- Styling tip or feature highlight

### Friday: Weekend essentials
- Suggest pieces for relaxed days

### Weekend: Customer spotlights
- User-generated content, reviews

## IMAGE SPECIFICATIONS

### File Formats
- Hero/main: PNG or WebP (high quality)
- Product cards: WebP (optimized for web)
- Social media: PNG for graphics, JPEG for photos

### Recommended Sizes
- Hero banner: 1600-2000px wide
- Product thumbnails: 400-600px wide
- Instagram post: 1080x1080px
- Instagram story: 1080x1920px
- Website icons/badges: 100-200px square

### Aspect Ratios
- Hero banners: 16:9 or 4:3
- Product cards: 1:1 or 4:5
- Instagram: 4:5 or 1:1 (posts), 9:16 (stories)
- Collection pages: vary, maintain consistency
